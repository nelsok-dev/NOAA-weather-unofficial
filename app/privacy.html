Privacy Policy

NOAA Weather Unofficial — Effective August 1, 2026

This is an unofficial app. It is not created, endorsed, or sponsored by NOAA, the National Weather Service, NASA, or any U.S. government agency.

In short: there is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. The app has no server that stores anything about you — with one exception: if you turn on push notifications, a small alert relay we operate has to keep your device's notification token and the location(s) you want alerts for, because that is the only way to know which device to warn when the National Weather Service issues something for your area. Turning notifications off deletes that record.

Location Data

When your location is read

Location access is optional and always requested through the standard iOS permission prompt. If you grant it, the app reads your device's location periodically while you are using it — not only when you tap the location button. It may read your position when the app starts or returns to the foreground, when it refreshes alerts, and to keep the home screen widget current. Readings are cached for about 20 minutes so the app is not continuously polling your position.

The app does not use background location and cannot read your position while it is closed. Severe-weather alerts arrive by push notification, which does not require background location.

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings. The app remains fully usable — you can search for any city or ZIP code instead.

Where your location is sent

Your coordinates are sent to the public data services listed under “Third-Party Services” below to look up the forecast, alerts, tides, marine conditions, and air quality for that spot. Those requests are made directly from your device. Apart from the alert relay described next, we do not operate any server that receives or stores your location.

Push Notifications & the Alert Relay

Push notifications are opt-in. If you enable them, the app registers with the NOAA Alert Relay, a small server we operate at noaa-alert-relay.nelsok.workers.dev, hosted on Cloudflare Workers.

The relay periodically checks the NWS public alert feed and sends a push through Apple's APNs service when an active alert covers one of your locations. It does not receive your name, email address, contacts, device identifiers beyond the APNs token, or anything tied to a user account. It is never used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Only your current locations are kept — no location history. Each update overwrites the previous values. Your record is deleted when you turn notifications off in the app, and automatically when Apple reports your device token as no longer valid (uninstalling the app, or disabling notifications at the system level).

Opt-out. Turn off the notifications switch in Settings, or disable notifications in iOS Settings → NOAA Weather Unofficial → Notifications.

Stored on Your Device

The following is kept locally and never transmitted to us:

  • Your saved locations and the currently active one
  • Your settings — units, quiet hours, severity filter, briefing time
  • Recent alert history and which alerts you have already seen
  • Cached weather and map data, so the app works briefly without a connection
  • The random installation identifier described above

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Home Screen Widget

The optional home screen and Lock Screen widgets read your saved location and unit preferences from a storage area shared with the app, and fetch forecast data directly from the National Weather Service API to refresh. The widget sends nothing to us and has no access to your location beyond what the app has already stored.

Requests are made only when the corresponding feature is used — tide and marine services are contacted only for coastal locations, and the alert relay only if you have enabled push notifications. Cloudflare processes relay traffic under its own privacy practices.

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking software of any kind.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No account, sign-in, name, or email address.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no participation in Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework — the app collects no tracking data.
  • No advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No cookies or cross-app tracking technologies.
  • No location history, search history, or behavioural profile.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared for marketing purposes. Ever.

Children

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. It requires no account and collects no personal identifiers from users of any age.
