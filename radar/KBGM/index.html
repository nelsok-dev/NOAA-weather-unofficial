KBGM radar: Binghamton, NY

Live NEXRAD Doppler radar loop from the National Weather Service radar at Binghamton, New York. The loop updates every few minutes, straight from the NWS.

Open the interactive radar

The interactive radar has a 3-hour loop you can zoom and pan, lightning, NWS warning polygons, and Storm Prediction Center outlooks. If you have the iPhone app, the Open button in Safari's banner at the top of this page shows this radar in the app.

This radar is run by the NWS forecast office BGM. Office website · KBGM on radar.weather.gov
